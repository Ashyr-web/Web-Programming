About Engineering College

Engineering College is committed to delivering high-quality engineering education that blends theory and practice. Our faculty are experienced professionals and researchers dedicated to student success.

We focus on interdisciplinary collaboration, ethical engineering, and preparing graduates to solve real-world problems with competence and leadership.

Computer Engineering

Duration: 4 years

Key subjects: Algorithms, Operating Systems, Networks.

Electrical Engineering

Duration: 4 years

Key subjects: Circuits, Power Systems, Control Systems.

Mechanical Engineering

Duration: 4 years

Key subjects: Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Materials.
